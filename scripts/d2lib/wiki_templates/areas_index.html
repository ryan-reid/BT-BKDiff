How This Is Calculated

Generated from BK's levels.txt and monstats.txt. Hell area level uses MonLvlEx(H) when present, then falls back to MonLvl(H). Champions are area level +2, uniques +3 — so top-tier capable means uniques can reach level 90 or higher. Density is MonDen(H), useful as a relative signal rather than an exact count. Chunk data comes from lvlmaze.txt when available. Super chest potential is derived from objects.txt, lvlprest.txt, and DS1 layout files.
