# Mace Runewords

### Revenge
* **Runes:** Thul + Ort + Ral
* **Base Items:** Mace
* **Properties:**
    * \+20% Increased Attack Speed
    * 33% Chance to cast level 8 Nova on striking
    * 33% Chance to cast level 8 Fire Ball on striking
    * 33% Chance to cast level 8 Frost Nova on striking
* **Properties from Runes:**
    * **Thul:** Adds 3\-14 Cold Damage
    * **Ort:** Adds 1\-50 Lightning Damage
    * **Ral:** Adds 5\-30 Fire Damage
