# Grimoire Runewords

### Vigilance
* **Runes:** Dol + Gul
* **Base Items:** Grimoire
* **Properties:**
    * 5% Chance to cast level 35 Ring of Fire when struck
    * \+10% Faster Run/Walk
    * \+30% Faster Block Rate
    * \+50\-75 to Life
    * \+50\-75 to Mana
    * All Resistances \+25\-35
    * \+75\-100% Enhanced Defense
* **Properties from Runes:**
    * **Dol:** \+3 Life after each Kill
    * **Gul:** \+5% to Maximum Poison Resist; \+2% to Experience Gained
