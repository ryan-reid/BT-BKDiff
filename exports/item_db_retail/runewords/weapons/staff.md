# Staff Runewords

### Heart of the Oak
* **Runes:** Ko + Vex + Pul + Thul
* **Base Items:** Staff
* **Properties:**
    * \+40% Faster Cast Rate
    * Level 25 Oak Sage \(4/\# Charges\)
    * Increase Maximum Mana 15%
    * \+3 to All Skills
    * Replenish Life \+20
    * All Resistances \+30\-40
    * Level 60 Raven \(14/\# Charges\)
* **Properties from Runes:**
    * **Ko:** \+10 to Dexterity
    * **Vex:** 7% Mana stolen per hit
    * **Pul:** \+100 to Attack Rating against Demons; \+75% Damage to Demons
    * **Thul:** Adds 3\-14 Cold Damage

### Leaf
* **Runes:** Tir + Ral
* **Base Items:** Staff
* **Properties:**
    * \+3 to Fire Skills
    * \(2\.0 per clvl\) Defense \(Based on Character Level\)
    * Cold Resist \+33%
    * \+3 to Inferno \(Sorceress only\)
    * \+3 to Fire Bolt \(Sorceress only\)
    * \+3 to Warmth \(Sorceress only\)
* **Properties from Runes:**
    * **Tir:** \+2 to Mana after each Kill
    * **Ral:** Adds 5\-30 Fire Damage
