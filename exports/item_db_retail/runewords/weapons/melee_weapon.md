# Melee Weapon Runewords

### Eternity
* **Runes:** Amn + Ber + Ist + Sol + Sur
* **Base Items:** Melee Weapon
* **Properties:**
    * \+260\-310% Enhanced Damage
    * Indestructible
    * Slows Target by 33%
    * Level 88 Revive \(8/\# Charges\)
    * Replenish Life \+16
    * Regenerate Mana 16%
    * Cannot Be Frozen
* **Properties from Runes:**
    * **Amn:** 7% Life stolen per hit
    * **Ber:** 20% Chance of Crushing Blow
    * **Ist:** 30% Better Chance of Getting Magic Items
    * **Sol:** \+9 to Minimum Damage
    * **Sur:** Hit Blinds Target \+1
