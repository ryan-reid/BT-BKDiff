# Axe Runewords

### Beast
* **Runes:** Ber + Tir + Um + Mal + Lum
* **Base Items:** Axe
* **Properties:**
    * \+40% Increased Attack Speed
    * Level 9 Fanaticism Aura When Equipped
    * \+240\-270% Enhanced Damage
    * \+25\-40 to Strength
    * Level 5 Summon Grizzly \(13/\# Charges\)
    * \+3 to Werebear
    * \+3 to Lycanthropy
* **Properties from Runes:**
    * **Ber:** 20% Chance of Crushing Blow
    * **Tir:** \+2 to Mana after each Kill
    * **Um:** 25% Chance of Open Wounds
    * **Mal:** Prevent Monster Heal
    * **Lum:** \+10 to Energy

### Crescent Moon
* **Runes:** Shael + Um + Tir
* **Base Items:** Axe
* **Properties:**

    * Magic Absorb 9\-11%

* **Properties from Runes:**
    * **Shael:** \+20% Increased Attack Speed
    * **Um:** 25% Chance of Open Wounds
    * **Tir:** \+2 to Mana after each Kill
